FAQ about supplying and exporting Egyptian vegetables

What is the difference between the local price and the export price?

The local price in EGP covers supply inside Egypt only. The export price in USD is set under FOB or CIF terms and depends on season, order volume and port of destination — it is never tied to the local market price.

What is the minimum export order?

It starts from one 20-foot container for potatoes and onions, one 40-foot container for tomatoes and oranges, and is made to order for perishables such as cucumber, zucchini and green beans.

Do you provide export certificates?

Yes. We supply under GlobalGAP with phytosanitary, veterinary and health certificates, together with the agreed packing format and cold chain.

Do you supply restaurants and retailers?

Yes. We provide weekly supply for restaurants, retailers and wholesalers, at wholesale prices according to the grade and the requested size.
